About

About Zoe Life

Helping people thrive in every season of life.

Zoe Life was founded by Pastors Tayo and Kemi.

The name Zoe comes from the Greek word used in John 10:10 for the abundant life Jesus came to give. That is the heart of this work, said in ordinary language: helping people thrive in every season of life.

You don't have to do life by yourself. We care about people, and we want this to be a place where you can start a conversation, pick up a resource, and keep going, whether you already walk with God or you are simply looking for a little more hope.

We believe biblical truth is not boxed into one corner of life. It gives wisdom for relationships, family, school, work, and the questions that show up on ordinary days.

Every season has room to grow. Some seasons also bring questions, transitions, and hard days that are easier to walk through with encouragement and practical support.

Through books and resources, speaking, workshops, and personal conversations, Zoe Life seeks to encourage individuals, couples, families, students, and professionals with biblical principles and tools they can use in everyday life.

We start with Scripture, then talk about what it means for the life you are actually living. Every person's journey is different. Our desire is to meet people where they are and help them move forward with wisdom, faith, and purpose.

Mission

What we are here for.

Zoe Life equips individuals, couples, families, churches, and organizations with biblical truth, practical wisdom, and Christ-centered resources to help them thrive in every season of life.

We start with what Scripture says, then talk it through in spoken English for real decisions, real relationships, and real seasons.

Vision

Lives changed by the truth of God's Word.

We want to see people growing, marriages strengthened, families flourishing, leaders serving with wisdom, churches equipped, and communities blessed by people who live what they believe.

Who we are

Core values

Christ-centered

Jesus Christ is the foundation of everything we do. We desire that every resource, conversation, and opportunity to serve points people toward Him.

Biblical truth

God's Word is our final authority. We seek to faithfully understand, teach, and apply Scripture in ways that are both biblically sound and practically relevant.

Practical wisdom

Knowledge is most valuable when it is applied. We are committed to guidance that helps people live out biblical principles in everyday life.

Excellence

We seek to honor God by pursuing excellence in our work, our relationships, our communication, and the resources we create.

Integrity

We desire to serve with honesty, humility, transparency, and faithfulness, reflecting the character of Christ in all we do.

Compassion

Every person has a unique story. We strive to serve with grace, patience, understanding, and genuine care.

Stewardship

We believe every gift, opportunity, and resource entrusted to us should be managed faithfully for God's glory and the benefit of others.

Growth

Spiritual maturity is a lifelong journey. Our desire is to encourage continual growth in faith, wisdom, character, and service.

Meet Tayo and Kemi

Tayo and Kemi Akinyemi are husband and wife, and the founders of Zoe Life. They share a passion for helping people grow and thrive. Their work is shaped not only by what they have studied and taught, but also by what they have lived.

They also serve as pastors at Life Springs Church. Through Zoe Life they bring together their faith, their work, years of teaching and mentoring, and lessons learned through marriage, parenting, and everyday life.

Statement of faith

Grounded in historic Christian faith.

At Zoe Life, our work is grounded in historic Christian faith and the authority of God's Word. We believe:

  • The Bible is the inspired, authoritative, and trustworthy Word of God.
  • There is one God, eternally existing as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  • Jesus Christ is the Son of God, fully God and fully man, whose life, death, and resurrection provide salvation for all who place their faith in Him.
  • Salvation is by God's grace through faith in Jesus Christ and cannot be earned by human effort.
  • The Holy Spirit indwells every believer, empowering holy living, spiritual growth, and faithful service.
  • Every person is created in the image of God and possesses inherent dignity and value.
  • The Church is the body of Christ, called to worship God, make disciples, and demonstrate His love to the world.
  • Marriage is God's covenant between one man and one woman and is designed to reflect Christ's relationship with His Church.
  • God calls believers to lives of holiness, integrity, compassion, stewardship, and faithful obedience.
  • Jesus Christ will return, and God's Kingdom will be fully established according to His promises.

Everything we teach, create, and provide through Zoe Life seeks to be consistent with these biblical convictions.
